Privacy,
plainly.

This portfolio is a small, static website. You can browse it without filling out a form or creating an account.

What information I collect

You can browse this site without giving me personal information. There is no form or account on the site.

If you email me, I receive the details you choose to include—such as your email address and message—in that email conversation. Your email app and the email providers involved handle the message.

Your browser also connects to the service hosting this page. That service may keep basic technical logs, such as connection time or browser details. I don’t use this website to view or track those visits.

How I use your information

If you contact me, I use your message to reply and discuss what you asked about. I don’t collect details through this site for advertising or sell them.

Other websites and services

This portfolio links to Instagram, X, and the Semi’s Cuts project website. Those sites are not embedded here. If you follow a link, that site’s own privacy practices apply.

Email links open your email app; the email services used to send and receive your message handle that information.

How I protect your information

This site does not keep a database of visitor details or contact form submissions. Messages you send are handled as email, so avoid including sensitive information you don’t need to share.

Your rights

You can choose not to email me or follow external links. You can also ask me to share, correct, or delete information in an email conversation that I control. Your email provider may keep its own copies.
